About this page

This is a free interval timer that runs in your web browser. Set the ready, work and rest seconds and the number of sets, and press Start. It beeps on the last three seconds of each phase, sounds a tone at each change, and a longer one at the end. There is nothing to install and no account.

Work can be 5 to 300 seconds, ready and rest 5 to 120 seconds, and a workout 2 to 40 sets. It starts on 10 seconds ready, 20 seconds work, 10 seconds rest and 8 sets. There is no rest after the last set. Your settings are written into the address of the page, so you can bookmark a timer or send it to someone.

A browser has limits, and it is better to say so. This timer needs the screen on and this tab in front. If the phone locks, or you switch to another app or tab, the beeps stop or come late; the countdown itself is correct again when you come back. The page asks the browser to keep the screen awake while the timer runs, where the browser allows it.

What the app adds

This page is the small sibling of Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer, an app for iPhone and Android by the same developer. The app does what a browser cannot do reliably:

  • It keeps running with the phone locked or another app in front.
  • A spoken countdown, in a female or male voice, plays over your music. On iPhone you can choose beeps instead.
  • Up to five saved timers on iPhone.
  • Workout history.

It is free, with one small banner ad and no account.

What is Tabata training?

After a warm-up, you alternate 20 seconds of all-out effort with 10 seconds of rest, eight times — four minutes in total. The protocol is named after Professor Izumi Tabata of Ritsumeikan University, whose research on it was published in 1996.
