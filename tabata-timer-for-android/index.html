A free Tabata timer for Android

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer is on Google Play for Android 8.0 or later. It opens on the standard Tabata protocol, 20 seconds of work and 10 seconds of rest for 8 sets, and a voice counts each change. It is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account.

How to use it

  1. Install it from Google Play and open it. The standard Tabata numbers are already set.
  2. Press start. Ten seconds of ready time run first.
  3. To change the numbers, open Settings and set the work time, the rest time and the number of sets.

What it does not do

  • No intervals longer than 60 seconds, so three-minute rounds cannot be set on Android.
  • No beeps, for now. The cues are a voice.
  • No Wear OS app.
  • No custom sequences, exercise library or workout plans.

Several Android apps are called "Tabata Timer". This one is by Takahiro Torii and its package is com.tabatatimer.

Questions

Is there a free Tabata timer for Android with no subscription?

Yes. Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer by Takahiro Torii (package com.tabatatimer) is free on Google Play for Android 8.0 or later, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. It shows one small banner ad.

What can I set on Android?

Ready time, work time and rest time from 5 to 60 seconds each, and 2 to 20 sets. It opens on 10 seconds ready, 20 seconds work, 10 seconds rest and 8 sets, the standard Tabata protocol.

Does it keep running with the screen off on Android?

Yes. It keeps counting and speaking with the screen off or another app open.

Does the Android app have beeps?

Not yet. On Android the cues are a voice. The iPhone and iPad app has three beeps as well.

Is the Android app the same as the iPhone app?

It is the same timer by the same developer, but smaller for now: intervals up to 60 seconds, up to 20 sets, one setting and a voice only. On iPhone work time goes up to 300 seconds, with up to 40 sets, five saved timers and beeps.
