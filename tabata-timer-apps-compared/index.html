Tabata timer apps compared: which one suits which workout

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

There is no single best Tabata timer app. A plain timer suits a workout that repeats the same work and rest; a fuller one suits a session built from different intervals. This page puts six of them side by side. It is written by Takahiro Torii, who makes one of the six, Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer, and it says where the others suit better.

The short answer

  • The same work and rest repeated, with nothing to set up (Tabata, 20 seconds on and 10 off, eight times; simple HIIT): Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer, the app this site is about.
  • EMOM, AMRAP and For Time: SmartWOD Timer.
  • Intervals of different lengths, or a warm-up running into the main set: Seconds Interval Timer or Intervals Pro.
  • A timer on Apple Watch: SmartWOD Timer, Seconds Interval Timer and Intervals Pro. The app this site is about has none.

Where Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer fits

It is the narrow one. It opens on 10 seconds ready, 20 seconds work, 10 seconds rest and 8 sets, so a Tabata session starts in two taps with nothing to set. Work time goes up to 300 seconds, rest up to 120 seconds and sets up to 40, and five timers can be saved. It is on iPhone and iPad and on Android (8.0 or later), with the same ranges, voices and beeps on both.

Every feature is free. In the written App Store reviews, the reason people give most often for choosing it is that it is simple.

Where another app fits better

  • Sets of different lengths in one session, or named intervals: Seconds Interval Timer or Intervals Pro.
  • EMOM, AMRAP or For Time workouts: SmartWOD Timer.
  • Timing from an Apple Watch: SmartWOD Timer, Seconds Interval Timer or Intervals Pro.
  • An exercise library, videos or coaching: none of that is in Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer.

Questions

What is the best Tabata timer app?

It depends on the workout. For plain Tabata, 20 seconds of work and 10 seconds of rest eight times, Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer by Takahiro Torii opens on exactly those numbers and starts in two taps; it is free, with no subscription. For EMOM and AMRAP, SmartWOD Timer has those modes. For sessions built from intervals of different lengths, Seconds Interval Timer and Intervals Pro are made for that.

Which Tabata timer app is free with no subscription?

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer by Takahiro Torii: every feature is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account, and one small banner ad. The other apps on this page are free to download; the App Store pages of SmartWOD Timer and of Float Tech's Interval Timer describe subscriptions.

Which Tabata timer apps have an Apple Watch app?

SmartWOD Timer, Seconds Interval Timer and Intervals Pro say on their App Store pages that they run on Apple Watch. Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer has no Apple Watch app.

Is there a simple Tabata timer for both iPhone and Android?

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer by Takahiro Torii is on iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later) and on Android (8.0 or later). Both have the same ranges, five saved timers, a female or male voice and three beeps.
