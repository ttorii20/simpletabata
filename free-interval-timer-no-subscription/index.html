About the ad

The app is not ad-free. It shows one small banner at the bottom of the screen, and that banner is what pays for it. It never covers the timer, it never plays a video, and nothing appears between pressing start and the workout beginning. There is no paid version without the banner.

If you want a timer with no ad at all and nothing to install, the timer in your browser on this site has none.

What you get without paying

  • Ready time, work time, rest time and the number of sets
  • On iPhone: work 5 to 300 seconds, ready and rest 5 to 120 seconds, 2 to 40 sets, and up to five saved timers
  • On iPhone: a female voice, a male voice, or one of three beeps (sharp, soft, whistle)
  • Running in the background, with the screen locked, and over your music
  • A list of finished workouts, and fifteen colour themes on iPhone
  • On Android: each interval 5 to 60 seconds, 2 to 20 sets, a voice

What it does not do

  • No custom sequences, EMOM or AMRAP modes. Every set uses the same work and rest time.
  • No Apple Watch or Wear OS app.
  • No exercise library, videos, coaching or workout plans.

Timers that do these things are often the ones with a subscription. This one stays small, and free.

Questions

Is there a free interval timer with no subscription?

Yes. Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer by Takahiro Torii is free on iPhone, iPad and Android, with no subscription, no in-app purchases, no trial and no account. It is paid for by one small banner ad.

Does it have ads?

One small banner at the bottom of the screen. There are no full-screen ads and no video ads, and nothing interrupts a workout.

Can I pay to remove the ad?

No. There are no in-app purchases of any kind, so there is no paid version without the banner.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no account, no sign-up and no email address to give.

Are any features locked?

No. Every setting, sound and theme is available without paying. Five of the fifteen themes on iPhone are earned by finishing workouts, not bought.
