A free boxing round timer: three-minute rounds, one-minute rests

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer times boxing rounds on iPhone and iPad. A round can be up to five minutes and a rest up to two, for 2 to 40 rounds, so three-minute rounds with a one-minute rest can be set directly. It is free, with no subscription and no account.

How to set it

  1. Open Settings and set the work time to 180 seconds. That is one round.
  2. Set the rest time to 60 seconds.
  3. Set the number of sets to the number of rounds, for example 12.
  4. Press start. The ready time, 10 seconds by default, runs first.

The phone can go in a pocket or on the floor. The timer keeps running with the screen locked, and on iOS 16.1 or later the remaining time shows on the Lock Screen.

Work time can be 5 to 60 seconds in steps of five, 70 to 120 in steps of ten, or 150, 180, 210, 240, 270 or 300 seconds. Rest and ready time stop at 120 seconds.

Up to five timers can be saved on iPhone, so sparring rounds, bag work and skipping can each keep their own numbers. Swipe left or right on the main screen to switch.

Hearing it in a gym

Under Sound in Settings, choose a female voice, a male voice, or one of three beeps: sharp, soft or whistle. A beep carries further than a voice over bags and music. The sound plays at the phone's media volume, so a Bluetooth speaker makes it as loud as the speaker. Music keeps playing underneath at its own volume.

What it does not do

  • Every round is the same length, and every rest is the same length. A session with rounds of different lengths needs a fuller interval timer.
  • On Android each interval stops at 60 seconds, so three-minute rounds are iPhone and iPad only, for now.
  • There is no Apple Watch app.
  • The spoken cues are in English. The beeps have no language.

Questions

Can it time three-minute boxing rounds?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. Set the work time to 180 seconds and the rest time to 60 seconds, and set the number of sets to the number of rounds. Work time goes up to 300 seconds, rest up to 120 seconds, and there can be 2 to 40 rounds.

Is the boxing round timer free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. There is one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen and no full-screen or video ads.

Does it keep timing rounds with the screen locked?

Yes. It keeps counting and sounding in the background and with the screen locked. On iPhone with iOS 16.1 or later the remaining time and a stop button show on the Lock Screen.

Can I use it for MMA or grappling rounds?

Yes. A round can be up to five minutes (300 seconds) with a rest of up to two minutes.

Does the Android version time three-minute rounds?

Not yet. On Android each interval is 5 to 60 seconds and a workout is 2 to 20 sets.
