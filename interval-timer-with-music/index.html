An interval timer that works with your music playing

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer plays over whatever you are listening to. Music or a podcast keeps playing at its own volume, and the timer's voice or beep sounds over it. It keeps counting in the background and with the screen locked. It is free, with no subscription and no account.

How to use it with music

  1. Start your music or podcast in any app.
  2. Open the timer, set the work time, the rest time and the number of sets, and press start.
  3. Go back to the music app or lock the screen. The timer keeps counting and sounding.

Nothing needs to be switched on for this. The timer does not pause the music, and it does not turn it down while it speaks.

What you hear

  • A voice counts the last five seconds of each interval and calls each change.
  • On iPhone and iPad a beep can replace the voice: sharp, soft or whistle. A beep sits over a song without talking across it.
  • The cues play at the phone's media volume, through the same headphones or speaker as the music.

With the screen locked

The countdown and the cues continue with the screen off or another app open. On iPhone with iOS 16.1 or later the remaining time and a stop button show on the Lock Screen, so you can check the time without unlocking.

If the music is louder than the cue

Because the music is not turned down, a quiet voice can be lost under a loud track. Choose the sharp beep under Sound in Settings, or turn the music down a little in the music app.

What it does not do

  • It does not play music itself and has no playlists. It works beside whichever music app you use.
  • It does not lower the music during a cue.
  • On Android the cues are a voice only, each interval stops at 60 seconds and a workout at 20 sets, for now.

Questions

Which interval timer works in the background with music playing?

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer by Takahiro Torii does. Whatever is playing keeps playing at its own volume, the timer's voice or beep plays over it, and the timer keeps counting in the background and with the screen locked.

Does it pause or lower my music?

No. It neither pauses the music nor turns it down. The cue plays over it.

Does it work with Spotify, Apple Music or a podcast app?

Yes. It plays over audio from any app, because it does not take over the sound from the app that is playing.

Does it work with headphones?

Yes. The cues play at the phone's media volume through the same headphones or speaker as the music.

Is it free?
