A simple interval timer: four numbers and a start button

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer has four numbers: ready time, work time, rest time and the number of sets. Set them, press start, and a voice or a beep tells you when to go and when to stop. There are no workout plans, no videos and no account. It is free for iPhone, iPad and Android, with no subscription.

As a rest timer in strength training

The app repeats one work time and one rest time, so it suits sets done against the clock: 40 seconds of squats, 90 seconds of rest, again. The rest can be up to 120 seconds.

It does not wait for you. If your sets take a different time each round and you want to start the rest with a tap when you put the bar down, a rest timer built for that will suit you better.

Why it stays simple

  • It can be started in two taps, and you do not have to look at it again: the voice counts the last five seconds of every interval.
  • It keeps running with the screen locked and plays over your music.
  • In the written App Store reviews, the reason people give most often for choosing it is that it is simple.

What it does not do

  • No custom sequences, EMOM or AMRAP modes. Every set uses the same work and rest time.
  • No more than five saved timers.
  • No exercise library, videos, coaching or workout plans.
  • No Apple Watch or Wear OS app.

Questions

What is the simplest HIIT timer for iPhone?

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer by Takahiro Torii is built to be that: ready time, work time, rest time, the number of sets and a start button. It has no workout plans, videos, account or subscription, and it is free.

Can I use it without setting anything?

Yes. It opens on 10 seconds ready, 20 seconds work, 10 seconds rest and 8 sets, the standard Tabata protocol. Press start.

Can I use it as a rest timer between strength sets?

Yes, for sets done against the clock: it repeats one work time and one rest time, with rest up to 120 seconds. It does not wait for a tap at the end of a set, so it does not suit sets of varying length.

Can I save more than one timer?

Yes, up to five, on iPhone and on Android, and you swipe between them.

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. There is one small banner ad and no full-screen or video ads.
