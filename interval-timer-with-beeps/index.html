An interval timer with beeps or a whistle instead of a voice

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

On iPhone, iPad and Android, Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer can sound a beep instead of a voice. There are three beeps: sharp, soft and whistle. It is free, with no subscription and no account.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

How to choose a beep

  1. Open Settings and tap Sound.
  2. Tap a sound to hear it. There are five: a female voice, a male voice, and three beeps.
  3. Leave it on the one you want. The next workout uses it.

The five sounds

SoundSuits
Female voiceTraining alone, when hearing the numbers helps. Spoken in English.
Male voiceThe same, in a lower voice. Spoken in English.
Sharp beepLoud rooms and music
Soft beepHome, early mornings, shared spaces
WhistleTeams, classes and outdoor sessions

Why choose a beep

  • It carries across a gym and over loud music better than a voice.
  • It has no language. The voices speak English only.
  • It sits over a podcast or a song without talking across it. Whatever is playing keeps playing at its own volume.

What it does not do

  • The beep plays at the phone's media volume. For a hall or a field, connect a Bluetooth speaker.

The timer in your browser on this site also uses beeps, with nothing to install.

Questions

Can I have a beep instead of a voice?

Yes, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Under Sound in Settings you can choose a female voice, a male voice, or one of three beeps (sharp, soft, whistle), and tap one to hear it.

Is there an interval timer with a whistle sound?

Yes. Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer for iPhone, iPad and Android has a whistle among its three beeps.

Do the beeps play over music?

Yes. Whatever is playing keeps playing at its own volume, and the beep plays over it.

Is it free?

Yes. Every sound is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. There is one small banner ad and no full-screen or video ads.
