An interval timer for a class or a team practice

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer can run the clock for a whole group. Connect the phone to a Bluetooth speaker, choose a beep, press start and leave it on the bench: everyone hears when to go and when to stop. It is free, with no subscription and no account.

How to set it up for a group

  1. Connect the phone or iPad to a Bluetooth speaker.
  2. In Settings, under Sound, choose a beep. There are three: sharp, soft and whistle. Tap one to hear it through the speaker.
  3. Turn up the media volume on the phone and then on the speaker.
  4. Set the work time, the rest time and the number of sets, and press start.

The sound plays at the phone's media volume, so it is as loud as the speaker it is connected to. The app cannot make a phone louder than its own maximum, which is why a hall or a pitch needs a speaker.

Why a beep and not a voice

A beep cuts through shouting, bouncing balls and music better than a voice, and it needs no translation. The voices speak English; the beeps work for any group.

Work time goes from 5 to 300 seconds, rest from 5 to 120 seconds, and a session can be 2 to 40 sets. Up to five timers can be saved, so the warm-up, the circuit and the sprints can each keep their own numbers; swipe to switch between them.

During the session

  • The timer keeps running with the screen locked, so the phone can stay on the bench.
  • Music from the same phone keeps playing at its own volume, with the beep over it.
  • It works without an internet connection, in a gym with no signal or on a field.

What it does not do

  • Every set uses the same work and rest time. A session with a different length for each station needs a fuller interval timer.
  • There is no exercise library, and no list of names or scores.

Questions

Is it loud enough for a class or a team practice?

The cues play at the phone's media volume, so they are as loud as the phone or the speaker it is connected to. For a hall or a pitch, connect a Bluetooth speaker and choose a beep under Sound in Settings: a beep cuts through noise better than a voice.

Can the app make the phone louder?

No. It already plays at the phone's maximum media volume. To go louder, connect a Bluetooth speaker.

Does it have a whistle sound?

Yes, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Under Sound in Settings there are three beeps: sharp, soft and whistle.

Does it cost anything for a school or a club?

No. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. There is one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen and no full-screen or video ads.

Does it work on a field with no signal?

Yes. The timer, the voice and the beeps all work without an internet connection.
