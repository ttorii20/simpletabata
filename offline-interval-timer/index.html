An interval timer that works offline

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer · by Takahiro Torii · iPhone, iPad and Android · free

Tabata & HIIT Interval Timer works without an internet connection. The timer, the voice and the beeps all run offline, and there is no account to sign in to, so it starts the same way in a basement gym, on a field or in airplane mode. It is free for iPhone, iPad and Android.

What works without a connection

  • The timer itself: ready time, work time, rest time and sets.
  • The spoken countdown, in a female or a male voice.
  • The three beeps on iPhone: sharp, soft and whistle.
  • Running in the background and with the screen locked.

What a connection is used for

Only three things: the small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, anonymous usage statistics, and the workout history. None of them is needed to run a workout.

Nothing to sign in to

There is no account, no sign-up and no subscription to check, so nothing waits for a server when you press start. The app does not use location, contacts, the camera or the microphone.

Where that matters

  • Basement gyms and garages with no signal
  • Fields, tracks and trails
  • Travelling without a data plan
  • Airplane mode, to train without notifications

Questions

Does the interval timer work without an internet connection?

Yes. The timer, the voice and the beeps all work offline. A connection is used only for the banner ad, anonymous usage statistics and the workout history.

Does it work in airplane mode?

Yes. Nothing the timer needs comes from the network, so it counts and sounds the same in airplane mode.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-up, on iPhone, iPad or Android.

Does it keep running with the screen off?

Yes. It keeps counting and sounding in the background and with the screen locked.
